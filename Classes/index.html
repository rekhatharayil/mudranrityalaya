Classes

A structured path from first steps to the stage

Beginner

  • Gain a foundational understanding of Indian dance forms.
  • Learn basic Adavus (footwork) sequences.
  • Become familiar with Hastha Mudras (hand gestures) in coordination with Adavus and head and eye movements.
  • Learn Shlokas, introduced in a simple, student-friendly way.
  • Understand the importance of music, rhythm and tempo in Bharatanatyam.
  • Practice foundational exercises to improve posture, flexibility and coordination.

Intermediate

  • Master the complete repertoire of Adavus (footwork) and Hastha Mudras (hand gestures).
  • Learn to interpret and perform simplified Krithis (compositions) and Kavithuvams (devotional poems).
  • Gain experience in stage performances and productions.
  • Continue strength-building exercises.

Class Schedule

  • Weekly sessions, 60 minutes each
  • School year runs August to June
  • All classes are in person
  • Online classes available on request

Dance Forms

  • Classical Bharatanatyam for ages 5+
  • Semi-classical dance for adults
  • Group performance choreography for cultural events
